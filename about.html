Our Philosophy & Mission

Honoring the Hands That Cultivate Every Harvest

Bean & Brew was founded in 2018 with a singular guiding conviction: that coffee is not a mere commodity, but an artisanal craft connecting high-altitude growers to mindful drinkers across the globe.

Founded in Portland, Oregon

From a 1-Kilo Sample Roaster to a Vibrant Specialty Sanctuary

What began inside a drafty garage on a restored cast-iron sample roaster has grown into an internationally celebrated specialty brand. Founders Maya Lin (mechanical engineer turned roastmaster) and Julian Vance (licensed SCA Q-Grader) spent years trekking through the cloud forests of Yirgacheffe and the steep terraces of Huila.

They realized that industrial commercial coffee stripping all individuality from beans was a tragedy. By creating a direct micro-lot supply chain, paying farmers directly without intermediary brokers, and roasting each terroir to highlight its native floral, fruit, or chocolate profile, Bean & Brew was born.

"When you brew coffee roasted with genuine respect for its altitude and harvest, you do not need sugar or masks. You taste the rain, soil, and sunlight of distant volcanic slopes."
— Maya Lin, Founder & Head Roaster
Traceability & Mastery

The Farm-to-Cup Journey

Every single bean we roast passes through six rigorous checkpoints before touching your cup.

Stage 01 • High-Altitude Cultivation

Shade-Grown Heirloom Varietals

Grown above 1,700 meters under native canopy trees. The slow atmospheric maturation concentrates sugars, complex organic acids, and nuanced aromatics inside every cherry.

Stage 02 • Selective Hand-Harvest

Brix-Meter Ripeness Verification

Pickers harvest exclusively blood-red, fully ripe cherries. Every lot is checked with optical refractometers for 21%+ sugar content before immediate washing.

Stage 03 • Direct-Trade Partnership

Fair Remuneration & Community Funds

We bypass commercial export cartels. We guarantee multi-year contracts paying on average 45% above Fair Trade market price directly to farming cooperatives.

Stage 04 • Micro-Batch Thermodynamics

Precision Fluid-Bed Roasting

Using our zero-emissions Loring convection roaster, our roast profiles monitor rate of rise (RoR) within 0.1°C per second to prevent smoky carbonization and preserve floral volatiles.

Stage 05 • Sensory Lab Cupping

Licensed SCA Q-Grader Scoring (86+ Pts)

Every batch is cupped within 24 hours. Any roast falling below our exacting 86-point threshold is composted and never released to our shelves.

Stage 06 • Valved Eco-Packaging

Oxygen-Purged Compostable Bags

Sealed in 100% home-compostable Kraft barrier bags with one-way degassing valves to lock in fresh volatile aromas while letting CO2 safely vent.
