Barista Pro Tips

How to Keep Your Coffee at Peak Flavor

1. Oxygen Is The Enemy

Always reseal the bag tightly or transfer beans to an opaque vacuum canister. Never store beans in the refrigerator where condensation destroys lipids.

2. Grind Just Before Brewing

Coffee begins losing its delicate volatile aromatics within 15 minutes of being ground. Grinding whole bean fresh unlocks 80% more flavor.

3. Mind The Degassing Window

Coffee tastes best between day 4 and day 28 post-roast date. The initial degassing allows clean extraction without carbon dioxide channeling.
