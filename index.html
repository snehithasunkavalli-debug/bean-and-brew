The Bean & Brew Distinction

Elevating Everyday Rituals Into Extraordinary Experiences

Every bean tells a story of soil, elevation, climate, and relentless dedication. Here is how we preserve that journey in every cup.

🌱

Direct Farm Origin

We pay an average of 45% above Fair Trade minimums directly to indigenous growers in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Sumatra.

🔥

Micro-Batch Roasting

Our Loring Smart Roaster allows zero convection scorching and computer-monitored thermodynamic curves for pristine aromatic clarity.

⏱️

Peak Freshness Guarantee

Shipped within 48 hours of roasting in valved, oxygen-purged bags so you experience peak degassing aromatics at home.

🌍

100% Circular Footprint

From plant-based cornstarch cups to repurposed spent coffee grounds composted with local urban gardens, zero goes to landfill.

The Roastery Experience

A Warm Sanctuary for Coffee Purists & Dreamers Alike

Housed in a restored 1920s brick timber warehouse in the heart of Portland, Bean & Brew is equal parts production roastery, culinary sanctuary, and community hub. Watch our cast-iron roasters spin while you savor slow pour-overs brewed on pristine Hario V60 stations.

✓ Sunlit courtyard patio & cozy leather fireside seating
✓ Weekend sensory cupping flights & barista masterclasses
✓ Full plant milk bar (House Oat, Almond, Macadamia) at zero upcharge
