The Science of Extraction

Our Barista Brew Calibration Parameters

We calibrate our water TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) to 120-140 ppm and dial in grind microns every morning.

⏱️

Hario V60 Pour-Over

Ratio: 1:16.5 | Temp: 93°C / 200°F

Yields highest floral clarity and citrus brightness.
⚡

Nine-Bar Espresso

Ratio: 1:2.1 | Extraction: 27-30s

Concentrated crema, intense chocolate body and sweet finish.
🧊

Nitro Cold Extraction

Steep: 20 Hours | Temp: 4°C / 39°F

Velvety nitrogen cascade with ultra-low perceived bitterness.
🫖

French Press Immersion

Ratio: 1:15 | Steep: 4 Minutes

Preserves natural essential coffee oils for maximum rich mouthfeel.
